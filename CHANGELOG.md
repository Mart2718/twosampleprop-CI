# Changelog

## v1.4 — 2026-10-19
- The Compare Two Means steps now use Art of Stat's labels: the Confidence Interval & Significance Test tab, Summary Statistics, two-sided, and the equal variances box left unchecked.
- The Step 3 inputs card for means follows the app's order.
- The video title now reads "Art of Stat 2 Sample T-Test."

## v1.3 — 2026-10-19
- The Compare Two Proportions steps now use Art of Stat's labels: the Confidence Interval & Significance Test tab, Number of Successes, and Type of Interval: Wald.

## v1.2 — 2026-10-19
- Switched to the Santa Ana College palette (red, black, grey, white) on a light background.
- Activity zones are marked by shape and label: Your turn, In Art of Stat, How it works, Teacher only.
- Step 3 has an A–E step tracker, with the problem data pinned beside the work on laptop screens.
- The step tabs show progress.
- Feedback uses ✓/✗, borders, and words, and red no longer means "wrong."
- Fixed sideways scrolling on phones; scrolling tables can now be reached with the keyboard.

## v1.1 — 2026-10-19
- Added a z/t critical-value curve: the middle C% is shaded, ±z* or t* are marked, and earlier levels show as faint outlines.
- Added a "Type these into Art of Stat" card for each problem, with Copy buttons.
- Added "How Art of Stat got this," which walks from estimate to SE, critical value, and margin of error.
- Part E shows the intervals at each level stacked on one number line.
- Step 2 has a resource link bar and an optional "Connect to hypothesis tests" section.
- WCAG pass: skip link, heading order, keyboard tabs, non-color cues. Fixed all three steps showing at once in the standalone file.

## v1.0 — 2026-10-18
- First release: 12 procedure scenarios, Art of Stat how-to, 6 compute-and-interpret problems with feedback that names the likely mistake, Teacher mode and Class mode.
